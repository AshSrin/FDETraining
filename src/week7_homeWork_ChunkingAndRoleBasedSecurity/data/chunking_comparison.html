Recommendation: markdown-aware chunking. It is the only strategy where every chunk carries its section heading path and none start mid-sentence, it is as fast as fixed-size, and it ties or leads on retrieval. Semantic chunking has similarly clean boundaries but needs an embedding pass at chunking time and can separate a heading from the text it introduces (see its poor boundary below).
